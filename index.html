About us

From the classroom to real engineering

We are Emirati engineering students at the Higher Colleges of Technology, turning what we learn into practical engineering experience.

Our goal is to contribute to the UAE's progress in research, innovation and emerging technology. The KR8.063 Nanodrone project builds the skills and technical capability that the nation's technological future depends on.

We develop and test on the Crazyflie 2.x research platform while we design and build our own custom nano drone.

Modular development

Platforms, sensors and technologies are tested, combined and expanded step by step as the research progresses.

Research-driven design

Engineering decisions rest on technical studies, academic literature and continuous evaluation.

Multidisciplinary engineering

Mechanical, hardware, software and AI teams build one integrated indoor nanodrone platform together.

Open-source innovation

Open-source tools speed up prototyping, so the team can focus on system integration, algorithms and autonomy.
